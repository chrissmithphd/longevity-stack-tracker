# Longevity & Exercise Glossary

Definitions for terms used in your longevity routine, based on scientific research and longevity science consensus (as of January 2025).

---

## Exercise & Cardio Terms

### Zone 2 Cardio
**What it is:** Aerobic exercise at a moderate, sustainable intensity where you can still hold a conversation but are slightly breathless. Scientifically, it's ~60-70% of maximum heart rate or ~70-80% of lactate threshold.

**Why it matters for longevity:**
- Builds mitochondrial density and efficiency (more "cellular power plants")
- Improves fat oxidation (metabolic flexibility)
- Enhances cardiovascular health without excessive stress
- Supports healthy aging by maintaining aerobic capacity
- Low injury risk, sustainable for life

**How to know you're in Zone 2:**
- **Talk test**: You can speak full sentences but are slightly breathy
- **Heart rate**: Roughly 180 minus your age (±10 bpm variance)
- **Perceived effort**: 5-6 out of 10 (sustainable for 45+ minutes)

**Duration recommendation:** 45-60 minutes, 2-3x per week

---

### VO2 Max
**What it is:** The maximum rate at which your body can consume oxygen during intense exercise. Measured in milliliters of oxygen per kilogram of body weight per minute (mL/kg/min).

**Why it matters for longevity:**
- **Strong predictor of mortality**: Higher VO2 max = longer lifespan
- Declines ~10% per decade after age 30 (but can be improved!)
- Top 25% VO2 max = ~5x lower mortality than bottom 25%
- Protects against cardiovascular disease, metabolic syndrome

**How to improve:**
- High-intensity intervals (VO2 max training): 4-8 rounds of 3-4 min hard efforts
- Consistency: 1-2x per week maintains/improves VO2 max
- Zone 2 training builds the aerobic base that supports VO2 max

**Target zones:**
- Excellent (men 40s): >45 mL/kg/min
- Excellent (women 40s): >38 mL/kg/min

## Cellular & Molecular Longevity Terms

### Autophagy
**What it is:** The body's cellular "recycling program" - cells break down and remove damaged proteins, organelles, and other cellular debris.

**Why it matters for longevity:**
- Clears out dysfunctional mitochondria (mitophagy)
- Removes misfolded proteins that cause disease (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's)
- Enhances cellular stress resistance
- Declines with age - restoring it may extend healthspan

**How to activate:**
- **Fasting**: 16+ hours upregulates autophagy
- **Exercise**: Particularly endurance and strength training
- **Spermidine**: Dietary supplement that induces autophagy
- **Caloric restriction**: Mild, sustainable reduction
- **Sleep**: Deep sleep enhances autophagic clearance

**Key mechanism:** AMPK activation → mTOR inhibition → autophagy initiation

---

### Senescent Cells
**What they are:** "Zombie cells" that have stopped dividing but don't die. They accumulate with age and secrete inflammatory molecules (SASP - Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype).

**Why they matter for longevity:**
- Drive chronic inflammation ("inflammaging")
- Contribute to tissue dysfunction, frailty, age-related diseases
- Clearing them in mice extends healthspan by 20-30%
- Humans accumulate ~1-2% senescent cells per decade

**How to address:**
- **Senolytics**: Compounds that selectively kill senescent cells
  - Quercetin + Dasatinib (research compounds, not routine use)
  - Fisetin (higher doses may have senolytic effects)
- **Exercise**: Reduces senescent cell burden
- **Fasting**: May enhance senescent cell clearance

**Research status:** Promising animal data, human trials underway (2024-2025)

---

### NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide)
**What it is:** A critical coenzyme in every cell, essential for energy production (mitochondria) and DNA repair. NAD+ levels decline ~50% from age 20 to 60.

**Why it matters for longevity:**
- Required for sirtuin function (longevity enzymes)
- Powers mitochondrial energy production
- Activates DNA repair mechanisms
- Declining NAD+ = accelerated aging

**How to boost:**
- **NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)**: Direct NAD+ precursor, 250-500mg/day
- **NR (Nicotinamide Riboside)**: Another precursor, 300-500mg/day
- **Niacin (Vitamin B3)**: Less efficient but works
- **Exercise**: Naturally increases NAD+ production
- **Caloric restriction**: Preserves NAD+ levels

**Key pathways:** NAD+ → Sirtuins → Improved mitochondrial function + DNA repair

---

### Sirtuins (SIRT1-7)
**What they are:** A family of seven proteins (SIRT1-7) that regulate cellular health, stress resistance, and metabolism. Sometimes called "longevity genes."

**Why they matter for longevity:**
- **SIRT1**: DNA repair, mitochondrial function, inflammation reduction
- **SIRT3**: Mitochondrial health, protects against oxidative stress
- **SIRT6**: DNA repair, inflammation control, tumor suppression
- Activated by caloric restriction, fasting, exercise

**How to activate:**
- **NAD+ precursors**: Sirtuins require NAD+ to function
- **Resveratrol**: Activates SIRT1 (modest effect)
- **Fasting/CR**: Strong sirtuin activation
- **Exercise**: Upregulates sirtuin expression

**Mechanism:** Sirtuins deacetylate proteins → alter gene expression → longevity benefits

---

### mTOR (Mechanistic Target of Rapamycin)
**What it is:** A cellular sensor that detects nutrients (amino acids, glucose) and signals for growth and protein synthesis.

**Why it matters for longevity:**
- **High mTOR**: Growth, muscle building, anabolism (good when young)
- **Low mTOR**: Autophagy, longevity pathways (good for healthspan)
- **The balance**: Need mTOR for muscle, but chronic high mTOR accelerates aging

**How to modulate:**
- **Activate mTOR** (muscle building):
  - Protein intake (especially leucine)
  - Resistance training
  - Fed state

- **Inhibit mTOR** (longevity):
  - Fasting (16+ hours)
  - Caloric restriction
  - Rapamycin (research drug, not routine)
  - Polyphenols (resveratrol, EGCG)

**Strategy:** Cycle between mTOR activation (fed, training) and inhibition (fasting, rest days)

---

### AMPK (AMP-Activated Protein Kinase)
**What it is:** A cellular "energy sensor" that activates when energy is low (high AMP:ATP ratio). Acts as a master metabolic switch.

**Why it matters for longevity:**
- Activates autophagy and mitochondrial biogenesis
- Inhibits mTOR (shifts from growth to maintenance)
- Improves insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism
- Mimics benefits of caloric restriction

**How to activate:**
- **Exercise**: Particularly endurance and HIIT
- **Fasting**: Energy deficit activates AMPK
- **Metformin**: Diabetes drug, activates AMPK (off-label longevity use)
- **Berberine**: Natural compound, AMPK activator
- **Resveratrol**: Indirect AMPK activation

**Key pathway:** Low energy → AMPK activation → mTOR inhibition + autophagy + mitochondrial health

---

### Nrf2 Pathway (Nuclear Factor Erythroid 2-Related Factor 2)
**What it is:** A master regulator of antioxidant defense. When activated, Nrf2 triggers production of protective enzymes that combat oxidative stress.

**Why it matters for longevity:**
- Upregulates 200+ protective genes (antioxidants, detox enzymes)
- Reduces oxidative damage (major aging driver)
- Protects mitochondria from damage
- Declines with age → more oxidative stress

**How to activate:**
- **Sulforaphane**: From broccoli sprouts, potent Nrf2 activator (best evidence)
- **Curcumin**: Turmeric compound, activates Nrf2
- **Quercetin**: Flavonoid, modest Nrf2 activation
- **Exercise**: Mild oxidative stress → hormetic Nrf2 response
- **Intermittent fasting**: Stress response activates Nrf2

**Mechanism:** Stressors → Nrf2 activation → production of antioxidants (glutathione, SOD, catalase)

## Supplement-Specific Terms

### Senolytics
**What they are:** Compounds that selectively eliminate senescent cells without harming healthy cells.

**Examples:**
- **Quercetin + Dasatinib**: Most studied combo (research use)
- **Fisetin**: High-dose may have senolytic effects
- **Navitoclax**: Experimental drug, powerful senolytic

**Status:** Animal studies very promising, human trials ongoing. Not yet standard practice.

---

### Mitochondrial Biogenesis
**What it is:** The creation of new mitochondria within cells. More mitochondria = more energy, better cellular function.

**How to stimulate:**
- **Zone 2 cardio**: Gold standard for mitochondrial growth
- **HIIT**: Signals for both efficiency and quantity
- **Cold exposure**: Mild stress → mitochondrial adaptation
- **NAD+ boosters**: Support mitochondrial function
- **PQQ, CoQ10**: May support mitochondrial health

---

### Inflammaging
**What it is:** Chronic, low-grade inflammation that increases with age (inflammation + aging = inflammaging).

**Why it matters:**
- Drives most age-related diseases (CVD, dementia, cancer)
- Accelerates tissue damage and dysfunction
- Creates a "pro-aging" environment

**How to reduce:**
- **Exercise**: Potent anti-inflammatory
- **Omega-3s**: EPA/DHA reduce inflammatory markers
- **Polyphenols**: Resveratrol, curcumin, quercetin
- **Sleep**: 7-9 hours, deep sleep reduces inflammation
- **Stress management**: Chronic stress → inflammation

## Practical Application

### Daily Longevity Stack (Example)
**Morning (with food):**
- Vitamin D3 + K2 (fat-soluble, synergistic)
- Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)
- NMN/NR (NAD+ boost)

**Afternoon (with meal):**
- CoQ10 (mitochondrial support)
- Curcumin (Nrf2, anti-inflammatory)
- Resveratrol (sirtuin activation)

**Evening:**
- Magnesium (sleep, recovery)
- Sulforaphane (Nrf2, detox pathways)

**Exercise:**
- 2-3x Zone 2 (45-60 min) → mitochondria, metabolic health
- 1x VO2 max intervals → peak capacity
- 2-3x Strength → mTOR activation, muscle preservation
- Daily joint mobility → injury prevention, movement quality

**Fasting:**
- 16:8 intermittent fasting → autophagy, AMPK, mTOR inhibition
- Optional 24-48h quarterly → deeper autophagic cleanup
